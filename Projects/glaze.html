Project Overview

Glaze Bakery Shop is a bakery shop located in La Paz Bolivia. This website is used for ordering and tracking orders. Users are able to create their accounts, and browse through the menu and place orders. The website also has a contact us page where users can contact the bakery shop and users experience a smooth and easy ordering process.
